  <h3>The Enduring Farmer</h3>
  <p>For this project, I am exploring the theme of 'Old and New' by inserting contemporary figures into classical art compositions. My piece juxtaposes slices of classical masterworks with modern collage aesthetics to examine the continuity of human labor. By placing contemporary agricultural figures within traditional art contexts, I aim to highlight that while time and artistic mediums evolve, the fundamental nature of the farmer remains a constant, enduring archetype. Technically, I am utilizing Photoshop’s layering, masking, and adjustment tools to seamlessly blend these disparate elements, creating a cohesive visual bridge between historical and modern representations of work.</p>
